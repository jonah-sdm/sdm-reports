Executive Summary

The BTC March27 expiry Long Call Spread 

is a limited-risk options strategy that combines a long call option at a lower strike and a short call option at a higher strike (same expiry). This structure allows investors to actualize a bullish view on BTC while significantly reducing upfront cost when compared with buying an outright call. In exchange for cost efficiency, the upside is capped at the higher strike ($105,000) and thus, for the specific call spread highlighted above, the maximum gross P&L at expiry is $20,000 per unit ($200K total) and is attained if BTC expires at any price greater than $105,000.


This structure is well-suited for investors with a constructive medium-term view on Bitcoin, seeking a capital efficient way to gain exposure. The balance of medium-term expiry and moderately out-of-the-money strike provide what is effectively leveraged exposure to a sustained move higher in BTC, all the while still keeping a very clearly defined and bounded downside risk. Namely, the investor's downside is limited to the cost of the trade.
